# NC IDEA MICRO Grant Application — Fall 2026

**Deadline:** Monday, August 24, 2026 at 5:00 PM EST
**Submit online at:** https://ncidea.my.site.com/
**Award:** $10,000 (75% upfront, 25% at 2 months)
**Info sessions:** July 13-22 (may have passed — check ncidea.org for any remaining)

---

## Key Free-Form Questions to Draft

### COMPANY INFO
- [ ] **DESCRIPTION** (500 chars) — 1-2 sentence company description
- [ ] **ADVISORS** (800 chars) — Formal/informal business or technical advisors

### PROBLEM & SOLUTION
- [ ] **PROBLEM** (1,500 chars) — What problem are you solving? How severe?
- [ ] **SOLUTION** (1,500 chars) — How does your solution uniquely address this?
- [ ] **DEFENSIBILITY** (1,500 chars) — IP, innovation, technology, market advantage

### CUSTOMER & MARKET
- [ ] **CUSTOMER DISCOVERY** — Number of interviews conducted
- [ ] **CUSTOMER DISCOVERY DETAIL** (1,000 chars) — Describe one specific conversation
- [ ] **ADDITIONAL DISCOVERY** (1,000 chars) — Other research, what you still need to learn
- [ ] **CUSTOMER DESCRIPTION** (1,500 chars) — Ideal customer/early adopter at launch
- [ ] **CUSTOMER ACQUISITION** (1,500 chars) — How to acquire customers
- [ ] **REVENUE MODEL** (1,000 chars) — How you make money
- [ ] **MARKET OPPORTUNITY** (1,000 chars) — Revenue projections, path to $2M+ in 7 years
- [ ] **COMPETITION** (1,500 chars) — Competitors + differentiation

### TRACTION
- [ ] **MOMENTUM & TRACTION** (1,500 chars) — Progress in past 6 months, metrics

### GRANT REQUEST
- [ ] **IMPACT** (500 chars) — What impact will $10K + programming have
- [ ] **MILESTONES** (1,000 chars) — 3-8 specific, measurable milestones for 5-7 month grant period
- [ ] **USE OF FUNDS** (1,000 chars) — How you'll spend the $10K

### OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED
- [ ] **VIDEO** (3 min max) — Elevator pitch or product demo (highly encouraged for software)

### OTHER DATA FIELDS
- Company structure (LLC, Corp, etc.)
- Incorporation date
- NC county
- Business category/sub-category
- Target market
- Revenue to date (enter 0 if pre-revenue)
- Expected months to revenue
- Fundraising status
- Funding sources breakdown (founder contributions, grants, loans, etc.)

## Strategy Notes

- They explicitly say "evidence over polish" — show real customer conversations and learnings
- 32% of software MICRO recipients were generating revenue at time of application — pre-revenue software is normal
- Only 15% of MICRO recipients had prior equity investment
- They want to see you'll be competitive for SEED ($50K) within 2 years
- Side-hustle/part-time founders are competitive IF you plan to go full-time within 6-12 months
