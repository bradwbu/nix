### What Aeroby Does
Database Backup as a Service platform with three key differentiators:
1. **Zero-Trust / Air-Gapped Backups:** Written to isolated WORM (Write-Once-Read-Many) storage off the customer's main cloud account to mitigate ransomware risk
2. **Cross-Cloud & Multi-Engine Portability:** Standardized backup & restore across PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, and Redis on self-hosted VPS, AWS, GCP, or bare-metal
3. **Instant Restore Verification:** Automated daily "dry-run" restores inside isolated containers to prove backup integrity

### Target Customers (ICPs)
- **Bootstrapped SaaS & Tech SMBs:** Teams with 1-15 engineers lacking a dedicated DBA or DevOps lead
- **Agencies & MSPs:** Client managers needing a single dashboard for 50+ client database backup states
- **Self-Hosted Infrastructure Users:** Businesses on Hetzner, DigitalOcean, EC2 without managed RDS/Cloud SQL snapshots

### Pricing Tiers
- **Starter ($19–$29/mo):** Up to 3 databases, 100 GB storage, daily automated snapshots, 7-day retention
- **Pro ($79–$99/mo):** Up to 10 databases, 500 GB storage, continuous WAL streaming (PITR), 30-day retention, automated restore testing
- **Agency/Team ($249/mo+):** Unlimited databases, multi-region replication, custom retention rules, SSO, team permissions
- **Overage:** $0.03–$0.05/GB above tier limits
